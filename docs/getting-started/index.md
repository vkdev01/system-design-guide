# Start here

A design interview is not a test of whether you have heard of Kafka. It is a test of
whether you can take a vague problem, put numbers on it, choose a structure, and then
reason honestly about what happens when it breaks. That is a skill with a shape, and the
shape can be practised.

This section gives you the shape.

## The one idea to take away

Every design decision is a purchase. You pay in consistency, latency, operational
complexity, or money, and you receive throughput, availability, or simplicity somewhere
else. Candidates who can name the price sound senior; candidates who only name the
purchase sound like they read a blog post.

The rest of this guide is mostly a catalogue of prices.
